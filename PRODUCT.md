# PRODUCT.md — EarLab

Headphone test lab for the audio-obsessed. The visitor (new headphones, or
"is my left bud dying?") runs a sweep, checks channels, walks a hearing
threshold test, or loops burn-in noise — in under two minutes, fully offline.
Kotlin/Compose, M3 Expressive, minSdk 26, AudioTrack synthesis, zero assets.
